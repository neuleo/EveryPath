# Specification - Gamification, Leaderboards, and Social Sharing

## Overview
As part of Phase 2, this track introduces social and competitive elements to EveryPath, allowing users to compare their coverage progress against friends or local communities, and share completed areas.

## Goals
- Define a scoring or points system based on unique distance covered.
- Create a global and local (area-based) leaderboard.
- Allow users to generate shareable links for their planned routes or coverage maps.
- Implement social profiles.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Users have a public profile URL displaying their total coverage and badges.
- Users can view a leaderboard ranking users by coverage percentage of predefined regions (e.g., "Munich City Area").
- Users can generate a read-only link to share a specific route they generated.
